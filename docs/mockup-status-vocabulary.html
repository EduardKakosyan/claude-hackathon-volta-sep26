Option B: four normalized states, source wording on tap

One legend for the whole map. Provincial pins use a hollow ring to show they are "no advisory", not "tested clean".

Open

Advisory

Closed
HRM "Closed", or a provincial card whose title says closed, or an algae notice matching this beach's lake
Off-season
Supervision ended; no one is testing. Last known status shown on tap
